FIND YOUR TRAINING STYLE

Muscle Building

Moderate reps and steady progression to build muscle size.

Level: Intermediate | Duration: 45-75 min

Explore Program

Sample exercises: Bench Press, Pull-ups, Squats, Shoulder Press, Bicep Curls.

Tip: Train 4-5 days a week, eat enough protein and get good sleep. Results vary from person to person.

Fat Loss

Weight training and cardio along with a small calorie deficit.

Level: Beginner | Duration: 30-50 min

Explore Program

Sample exercises: Squats, Push-ups, Lunges, Planks, Brisk walking.

Tip: Go slow and steady. Extreme diets are hard to follow and not recommended.

Strength Training

Heavy compound lifts with low reps and long rest times.

Level: Advanced | Duration: 60-90 min

Explore Program

Sample exercises: Squats, Bench Press, Deadlifts, Shoulder Press.

Tip: Use a spotter for heavy lifts and rest 2-5 minutes between sets.

Beginner Fitness

Simple full-body workouts to learn the basic movements.

Level: Beginner | Duration: 30-45 min

Explore Program

Sample exercises: Lat Pulldown, Incline Dumbbell Press, Lunges, Planks.

Tip: Start with light weights, warm up first and train 2-3 days a week.

Home Workouts

Bodyweight routines that need little or no equipment.

Level: Beginner | Duration: 20-40 min

Explore Program

Sample exercises: Push-ups, Lunges, Planks, Leg Raises.

Tip: Make exercises harder by going slower instead of adding weight.

Athletic Conditioning

Power, speed and endurance training for sports.

Level: Intermediate | Duration: 40-60 min

Explore Program

Sample exercises: Deadlifts, Squats, Pull-ups, Interval runs.

Tip: Warm up well and keep hard running days away from heavy leg days.

MASTER EVERY REP

Bench Press

Target: Chest | Equipment: Barbell, bench | Level: Intermediate

View details

Steps: Lie on the bench, grip the bar slightly wider than shoulders, lower it to your mid-chest and press it back up.

Common mistake: Bouncing the bar off the chest.

Safety: Use a spotter for heavy sets.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Target: Upper chest | Equipment: Dumbbells, bench | Level: Beginner

View details

Steps: Set the bench to a slight incline, press the dumbbells up above your chest and lower them slowly.

Common mistake: Setting the bench too steep.

Safety: Pick a weight you can control.

Pull-ups

Target: Lats | Equipment: Pull-up bar | Level: Advanced

View details

Steps: Hang from the bar, pull your elbows down until your chin passes the bar, then lower slowly.

Common mistake: Swinging the body.

Safety: Use a resistance band if you are not strong enough yet.

Lat Pulldown

Target: Lats | Equipment: Cable machine | Level: Beginner

View details

Steps: Sit down, hold the bar wide and pull it to your upper chest.

Common mistake: Leaning back too much.

Safety: Do not pull the bar behind your neck.

Deadlifts

Target: Lower back, glutes | Equipment: Barbell | Level: Advanced

View details

Steps: Stand with the bar over your feet, bend down and grip it, then stand up straight keeping the bar close to your legs.

Common mistake: Rounding the back.

Safety: Start light and learn the form first.

Shoulder Press

Target: Shoulders | Equipment: Dumbbells | Level: Intermediate

View details

Steps: Hold the weights at shoulder height and press them overhead until your arms are straight.

Common mistake: Arching the lower back.

Safety: Tighten your core while pressing.

Lateral Raises

Target: Side shoulders | Equipment: Dumbbells | Level: Beginner

View details

Steps: Raise light dumbbells out to your sides up to shoulder height and lower slowly.

Common mistake: Swinging the weights.

Safety: Keep the weight light.

Bicep Curls

Target: Biceps | Equipment: Dumbbells | Level: Beginner

View details

Steps: Keep your elbows at your sides and curl the weights up, then lower slowly.

Common mistake: Swinging the body.

Safety: Do not use a weight that is too heavy.

Tricep Pushdowns

Target: Triceps | Equipment: Cable machine | Level: Beginner

View details

Steps: Keep your elbows by your sides and push the bar down until your arms are straight.

Common mistake: Letting elbows move forward.

Safety: Keep your wrists straight.

Barbell Squats

Target: Quads, glutes | Equipment: Barbell, rack | Level: Intermediate

View details

Steps: Place the bar on your upper back, sit down and back, then stand up by pushing through your feet.

Common mistake: Knees falling inward.

Safety: Use the rack safety bars.

Planks

Target: Core | Equipment: None | Level: Beginner

View details

Steps: Rest on your forearms and toes and keep your body in a straight line. Hold.

Common mistake: Hips sagging.

Safety: Stop when your form breaks.

Leg Raises

Target: Lower abs | Equipment: Mat | Level: Intermediate

View details

Steps: Lie on your back and lift your straight legs up, then lower them slowly.

Common mistake: Arching the lower back.

Safety: Bend your knees if your back lifts off the floor.

UNDERSTAND YOUR BODY

Chest

The chest muscles help you push things away from your body.

Read more

Exercises: Bench Press, Incline Dumbbell Press.

Tip: Keep your shoulder blades pulled back while pressing.

Back

The back muscles help you pull and keep good posture.

Read more

Exercises: Pull-ups, Lat Pulldown, Deadlifts.

Tip: Think about pulling with your elbows, not your hands.

Shoulders

The shoulders let you lift your arms in every direction.

Read more

Exercises: Shoulder Press, Lateral Raises.

Tip: Warm up your shoulders before heavy lifting.

Biceps & Triceps

Biceps bend the elbow and triceps straighten it.

Read more

Exercises: Bicep Curls, Tricep Pushdowns.

Tip: Do not forget the triceps, they are most of your arm.

Quadriceps & Hamstrings

The thigh muscles are used for walking, running and jumping.

Read more

Exercises: Barbell Squats, Lunges, Deadlifts.

Tip: Learn the movement with light weight first.

Core & Abs

The core keeps your spine stable and connects your upper and lower body.

Read more

Exercises: Planks, Leg Raises.

Tip: Breathe normally while holding a plank.

FUEL YOUR PERFORMANCE

Protein

Muscle recovery and growth

Found in chicken, fish, eggs, yogurt, lentils and tofu.

Carbohydrates

Energy and workout performance

Found in rice, oats, potatoes, bread, pasta and fruit.

Healthy Fats

Hormonal and general health support

Found in nuts, seeds, avocado, olive oil and fish.

Hydration

Maintaining physical performance

Drink water through the day and a bit more when you train or when it is hot.

Pre-workout

Eat 1-3 hours before training. Carbs with some protein work well, for example oats with a banana.

Post-workout

Have protein and carbs after training, for example eggs on toast with fruit, and drink water.

Healthy meal ideas

Egg and avocado bowl, chickpea salad bowl, salmon with rice and vegetables.

This is general information only and not medical or personal diet advice.

KNOWLEDGE BUILDS STRENGTH

Common Gym Mistakes

Lifting too heavy, skipping warm-ups and changing plans every week.

Read more

Pick a simple plan, write down your workouts and judge your progress over months, not days.

Progressive Overload

Slowly do a little more over time so your body keeps adapting.

Read more

You can add weight, reps or sets. Once you can do all your reps with good form, increase the weight a little.

Importance of Recovery

Your muscles adapt when you rest, not while you lift.

Read more

Try to sleep 7-9 hours, eat enough and take rest days every week.

Beginner Gym Guide

How to start with a simple plan and feel confident.

Read more

Train 2-3 days a week with basic movements: a squat, a push, a pull and a core exercise. Start light.

Importance of Proper Form

Good technique protects your joints and makes each rep count.

Read more

Lower the weight if your form changes during a set. Moving slowly helps you stay in control.

Myths About Muscle Building

Separating facts from common fitness myths.

Read more

You cannot burn fat from just one body part, and building muscle takes years of steady effort, not days.
